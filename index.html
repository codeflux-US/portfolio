About me

I'm a B.Sc. Information Technology student and a fresher, which means everything on this site comes from coursework, self-study and projects I built on my own time rather than from a job.

I like the whole path a piece of software takes: writing it, shipping it, and then reading what the data says about it. That is why I'm working towards three connected areas — software engineering, DevOps and data analytics — instead of picking one and ignoring the rest.

Most of my learning is hands-on. I pick a small problem, build a working version of it with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python or SQL, break it, and fix it. Automation and problem solving are the parts I enjoy most, and I'm steadily adding Linux, Git and cloud fundamentals to that base.

I'm looking for an internship or entry-level role where I can contribute to real work and keep learning from people who've done it longer than I have.
